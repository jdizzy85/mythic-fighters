# Mythic Fighters

A kid-friendly mythical-creature fighting game for the browser and iPad. Vanilla JS + canvas, no assets to download.

Play: https://jdizzy85.github.io/mythic-fighters/

## Features
- 10 mythical heroes, each with a Nitro special plus a unique Creature Ult
- 10 weapons held in the creature's hand (fists, sword, axe, spear, staff, hammer, shield, bow, boomerang, claws), each with its own damage, reach, speed and swing animation
- Weapon skills and creature ults with cooldown indicators on the HUD and touch buttons
- XP, hero levels, coins, a 7-stat upgrade screen and a weapon shop/armory (saved in localStorage)
- 30-level story in 6 chapters across 7 arenas, with a boss every 5 levels and enemy variants
- Duel vs AI and endless Survival modes

## Controls
Move A/D or arrows, Jump W/Up/Space, Attack J/Z, Kick K/X, Block S/Down/Shift, Dodge U/V,
Nitro I/C, Weapon Skill L, Creature Ult O, Pause P/Esc. On iPad use the on-screen buttons.
